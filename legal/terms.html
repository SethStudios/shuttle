UniAbuja Shuttle · Last updated 16 September 2026

1. The service

UniAbuja Shuttle (“we”, “us”) sells digital boarding passes for scheduled buses between University of Abuja Main Campus and Mini Campus, operating 6:00am to 6:00pm daily, Monday to Saturday. By creating an account or buying a ticket, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

2. Who may use the app

Students and staff of the University of Abuja with a valid account. You must provide true details at sign-up, keep your password secret, and never share your account, QR codes, or tickets. One account per person. We may suspend accounts used to impersonate others or evade payment.

3. Fares and fees

The fare shown before checkout is the full price you pay — no service fee or hidden charges in the app. A ₦1,000 monthly subscription is required to book; trip fares are charged per ride on top. Fares and subscription price may change; the price at the moment you pay applies. All amounts are in Nigerian Naira (₦).

4. Payment

Payment is processed securely by Paystack (card, transfer, or USSD where offered). Your ticket is issued only after our server confirms the payment with Paystack — a Paystack receipt alone, without in-app verification, is not a valid boarding pass. If you are charged but no ticket appears, use “I’ve paid — verify” in the app before paying again, then contact support with your payment reference.

5. Tickets, queue numbers, and validity

Each paid booking issues exactly one ticket with one queue number for the travel date and boarding window shown on the pass. Queue numbers restart every boarding window. A ticket is valid only for its stated travel date, only until scanned (single use), and only within 24 hours of purchase — the boarding window holds your place in the queue, it is not a separate expiry. Expired, used, cancelled, or unverified passes admit no one.

6. Boarding rules

Arrive at least 10 minutes early and listen for your queue number. Show the in-app QR to the conductor for scanning; screenshots or forwarded links may be rejected where they cannot be verified live. Boarding order follows queue numbers at the conductor’s direction. For everyone’s safety, the conductor may refuse boarding for overloading, misconduct, unsafe items, or any safety concern — that decision is final on the day.

7. Cancellations, delays, and refunds

If a scheduled bus cannot run, we will advise rebooking or a refund through support. Refunds, where due, go back through the original Paystack payment method and follow Paystack timelines — we cannot refund in cash at the terminal. Missed buses for any reason attributable to the passenger (late arrival, wrong window, invalid pass) are not refundable. Duplicate charges for the same trip, once confirmed, are refunded after investigation.

8. Prohibited conduct

One ticket admits one passenger. Forging, sharing, or reselling QR codes or tickets, using another person’s account, tampering with the app, or abusing staff or drivers may lead to immediate denial of boarding, ticket cancellation without refund, and account suspension or permanent ban. We may preserve records needed for investigation.

We may change routes, timetables, fares, or app features with reasonable notice in the app. Buses do not operate on Sundays unless expressly announced.
